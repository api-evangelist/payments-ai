Payments AI is the AI-native Merchant of Record and payment orchestration platform. On the Merchant of Record product, the merchant of record is the seller of record and handles sales tax and VAT registration, filing, and remittance for you. On the orchestration product, you stay the merchant of record and we route payments across your gateways.

**Areas:** Company · Payments · AI · FinTech · Merchant of Record · Payment Orchestration

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for payments-ai: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
